Holodeck · Training Program

Up-Rigger

The holodeck stays at its default until you pick a program at the arch. Then the grid gives way to an arena: a catwalk and a 24-inch ladder beam 108 feet over the floor, a ground rigger below building a bridle, another up-rigger on the beam across from you. You have a 150-foot rope, a progress capture device, and a job: get your leg of the bridle up, around the beam, mated, squared and checked — without dropping anything. Gravity is always on. Gravity is the opponent.

A 5 and a 10 shackled together. The 5 wraps the beam; the 10 swings under and its shackle mates to the eye of the 5. The ground rigger's bowline is a 12-inch loop in the eye of the 5.

Haul

Metres of rope per metre of arm travel. 1 is real hand-over-hand — about ninety pulls to the beam.

How fast the other up-rigger hauls once you are pulling together. Match it or the bridle drifts.

Knots

Full turns around the rail that count as a clove hitch. Two is the knot.

Hands

Audio

  1. Gear — pockets empty, then the harness: shoulders, chest clip, belt, leg clips. Helmet on. Everything else clipped into the harness. Radio check. Grab the rope and go.
  2. Rope — deploy it to the floor, clove hitch and a half hitch on the catwalk rail. Clip your 6' double lanyard to the lifeline or the top rail before you touch the beam.
  3. PCD — untie, keep hold of the rope (or stand on it), walk out to the sling, hang the PCD, feed the rope the right way.
  4. Haul — on the ground rigger's call: leg to the apex, together to the hook eye, then the load, matching the other rigger's pace.
  5. Beam — burlap out, wrap the beam, mate it (Pin · Rope · Cable · Shackle), burlap under, steel down, square up, check for fouls.
  6. Point check — radio down.
Grip
Hold the grip button to hold things — gear, any part of the rope, a leg of steel. Let go over the void and it goes in
Harness
Bring it to your chest to shoulder it, then trigger at the chest, the belly, and each thigh to clip it. Seven rings: back, chest, belly, both hips, both rear. Gear clips to any ring; the lanyard hangs off the back ring and its hooks park on the shoulder straps
Rope
It is real: 150 feet of it, every foot simulated. Hold it anywhere, let it swing, feel it go taut. Pull too hard against a taut line and it slips through your hand
Trigger
Act on what is under your hand: pick a program, dress a knot, hang the PCD, mate a shackle
Knots
Hold the rope end at the rail and circle your hand around the rail — the game counts the turns. Trigger to dress it
Haul
Grip the rope below the PCD and pull down, hand over hand
